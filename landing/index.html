Built for small operators

Everything a storefront needs, nothing it doesn't

No dashboards to babysit. Kiosk handles the plumbing — you handle the food, the stock, and the customers.

Orders straight to Telegram

Every order lands in your Telegram chat the instant a customer checks out — name, items, total, and delivery details, formatted and ready to act on.

Runs on the edge

Your menu is served from Cloudflare's global network. A customer in another country sees it load just as fast as one down the street.

Your own domain

Point a custom domain at your storefront on the Pro plan. No "made with Kiosk" badge, no shared subdomain — just your name.

Take payments online

Connect Stripe or Monobank and let customers pay before you even see the order — or keep it cash-on-delivery, your call.

Know what's selling

See which items move, which times get busy, and where orders come from — enough signal to plan stock without a spreadsheet.

Run more than one shop

On the Business plan, manage up to five storefronts — different menus, different Telegram bots — from a single account.
